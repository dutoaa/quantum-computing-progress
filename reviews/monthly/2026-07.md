## Outlook

The July 2026 literature suggests that AI–quantum computing is entering a systems-and-infrastructure phase. Rather than asking only whether quantum models can work, researchers are now asking how to design them automatically, train them robustly, benchmark them fairly, and secure them against misuse. If this trajectory continues, the next advances are likely to come from integrated toolchains that combine LLM-guided synthesis, formal verification, hardware-aware compilation, and experimental benchmarking on realistic devices. The most impactful results will probably be those that connect multiple layers of the stack—algorithm, architecture, error correction, and security—into reproducible end-to-end workflows.
